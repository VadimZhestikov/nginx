  Stage 22 proposal — r.queryParams (parsed query string)

  Expose r.queryParams as a read-only plain JS object containing the decoded query-string parameters. Parsing splits r->args on &, each pair on =, applying ngx_http_arg() style decoding. Multi-value

  Example: /path?foo=bar&count=3 → {foo: "bar", count: "3"}.
